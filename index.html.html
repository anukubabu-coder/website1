Nurturing Minds, Inspiring Innovation

Welcome to Gasa's mountain sanctuary for academic milestones. We combine rigorous traditional frameworks with modern technical solutions.

Bjishong Central School, located in Damji, Gasa Dzongkhag, was established originally as a primary school in the year 2007, and was later upgraded to a Central School and the only higher secondary school in the district. It has served the rural community for decades, officially operating as a comprehensive boarding facility for regional students.
